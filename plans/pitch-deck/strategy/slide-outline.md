## Slide 2: The Problem

**Headline**: AI writes half the code. Nobody governs it.
**Purpose**: Quantify the governance gap between AI generation and production
**Primary data point**: 46% of code is AI-generated; 1.7x more defects; <50% reviewed before commit
**Win themes**: Governance at the speed of generation, Deterministic rules for probabilistic outputs, Architecture drift is the silent killer
**One takeaway**: AI-generated code is measurably worse and nobody is checking it at the point of generation

## Slide 3: Why Now

**Headline**: The compliance clock is ticking
**Purpose**: Create urgency with regulatory deadlines and market timing
**Primary data point**: EU AI Act high-risk deadline: August 2026 (5 months); 7% global turnover penalty
**Win themes**: Every line has an author, Compliance is continuous not quarterly
**One takeaway**: Governance is moving from optional to mandatory -- the purchasing window is now

## Slide 4: The Solution
