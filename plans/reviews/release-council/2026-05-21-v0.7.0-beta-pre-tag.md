## Judge synthesis

**Verdict: ship-with-doc.**

The tag-time daemon-working claim is honest and verifiable from code state
alone. All §3–§11 hard gates are green (code-verified) or carry forward with
confirmed no-drift evidence. The 20 high-severity clawpatch findings all land
in JS/TS retirement-track packages outside the Rust binary that constitutes
the daemon-working claim; defer-with-issue is the appropriate disposition for
every one. Two operator-visible behaviour changes are undocumented; both are
one-line CHANGELOG/runbook additions, not code fixes.

The most material discovery from this pass is **positive**: commit `c8193511`
(today, 2026-05-21 10:51 AWST) materially hardened the DeepSec #1674 lineage-
anchor mint defect that the 2026-05-20 council pass had dispositioned
ship-with-doc. The new `verify_lineage_claim()` at
`crates/anvil-intercept/src/ipc.rs` enforces `peer_pid == claim.pid` and
overrides client-supplied `pid_starttime` with the daemon's own
`/proc/<pid>/stat` read before any value reaches
`SessionRegistry::register_with_lineage`. Four regression tests pin the
contract. The Known-Gaps entry for #1674 is now stale and should move to
Fixed.

**R1. RMCPF heading shape will silently skip `aps.items[]` reconciliation.**
`plans/modules/rust-mcp-full-port.aps.md` uses `### RMCPF-011:` (three-hash)
and `### RMCPF-012:` (three-hash) heading shapes (lines 309 and 382). The §13
perl walk in `docs/runbooks/v0.7.0-beta-release-runbook.md:537-545` regexes
`^#### '"$id"':` (four-hash) — both items will be silently skipped, leaving
their Status at `Merged` after the tag instead of advancing to
`Released/Shipped`. The runbook itself names this failure mode at
§13:578-582. **Fix at §13 time** by either (a) editing the headings to
four-hash before §13 runs, or (b) running the manual spot-check
`grep -rE "^- \*\*Status:\*\*  *Merged"` after §13 and hand-promoting these
two items. Reviewer source: adversarial-reviewer.
