## Summary

Product-surface **patch** on the `v0.7.0-beta` sit-on slate that makes Anvil's
existing signal **visible and exportable** — native read-only TUI dashboards,
SARIF 2.1.0 findings export, and new `anvil insights` views — without widening
the protection claim. The bulk of this work is already Merged on `main`; the
remaining work is cut hygiene and reconciliation, not implementation.

**Version framing (deliberate):** `scripts/release/assess.sh` mechanically
recommends the next minor (`v0.8.0-beta`). We override **down to a patch**
(`v0.7.3-beta`) to honour the Hotfix Iteration Plan's "no minor beta before six
weeks post-tag" rule (`v0.7.0-beta` shipped 2026-05-21). **Pass `--version
v0.7.3-beta` explicitly to both `assess.sh` and `prepare.sh`** so the override
is on the record.

**No Boring Week gate applies** — that gate covers the sit-on claim, not weekly
patches.
