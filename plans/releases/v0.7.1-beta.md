## What ships

`v0.7.1-beta` is the first Boring-Week patch tag on the `v0.7.0-beta`
daemon-working slate. It does NOT broaden the daemon-working claim
or re-open the protection-claim vocabulary — that surface is frozen
on `v0.7.0-beta`. `v0.7.1-beta` is a focused patch that closes one
user-visible regression (GH
[#1831](https://github.com/eddacraft/anvil-001/issues/1831)) plus
the structural hardening the post-release Council review flagged.

**Patch-tag claim** (applies the moment `v0.7.1-beta` is cut):
`anvil start --verify` reaches `protecting` when the intercept
daemon attests live enforcement for the current worktree on every
shipped platform (Linux, macOS, Windows), with operator-actionable
repair hints for every daemon-state that blocks promotion.

The parent tag's "Daemon-Working End-to-End Protection" claim is
preserved verbatim — `v0.7.1-beta` does NOT graduate the
`v0.7.0-beta` sit-on claim. That remains gated on Wave 5 Boring
Week observation.

## Risk + rollback

- **Risk:** the activation surface now performs an IPC round-trip
  on every `verify()` invocation when at least one MCP client is
  at `RestartHandshakeVerified`. The 500 ms budget bounds the cost;
  a hung daemon does not stretch `verify` measurably beyond the
  pre-`v0.7.1-beta` baseline.
- **Rollback path:** the `daemon_evidence` module is invoked from
  a single call site in
  `activation::diagnostic::verify_with_home`. Deleting that call
  reverts to the `v0.7.0-beta` behaviour without touching any wire
  shape. The `generated_at_unix` field is wire-additive and
  consumers tolerate `0` as "no anchor".
- **No JSON-schema-consuming fixture revert needed.** The
  `ActivationDiagnostic.daemon_attestation` field is internal-
  surface; no `--json` consumer outside the binary depends on it.
