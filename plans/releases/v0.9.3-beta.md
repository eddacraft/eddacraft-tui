## Theme

Patch on the v0.9 line for **daily-path honesty** (what anvil says matches what
it does) and the **Windows install / self-update path** for cargo-dist users.
Not a new product theme; not Graph Trust Surfaces / dashboard.

Sources: Morgan Deus findings (CIB-220..227 / #3510); Dave field report and
packs (CIB-228..245, 250..276, pack trust 252/254 / #3514); pre-250 promotions
