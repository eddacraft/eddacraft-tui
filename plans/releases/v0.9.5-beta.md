## Theme

Feature release on the v0.9 line: after an upgrade, owned MCP configs and live
children heal so typical sessions stay attached (pin if you do not want that),
plus one canonical project config surface and honesty fixes so install, protect
claims, and verify mean what they say. Not a new product minor; not settings UI
or Graph Trust Surfaces.
