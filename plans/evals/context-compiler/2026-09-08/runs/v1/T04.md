## Answer (summary)

`ImpactSummary.truncated` is one flag on the counts-only blast-radius summary. Its own comment in `crates/anvil-gctx-types/src/lib.rs` says it is true when either the affected-symbol set or the dependent-closure walk hit its node budget; the returned sets are then a deterministic path-ordered prefix, not a silent full cutoff. A consumer therefore cannot tell: (1) Which budget fired. The same bool covers two distinct caps. Egress sets one `truncated` in `collect_impact_with_budget` (`crates/anvil-gctx-egress/src/lib.rs`): it becomes true when the affected-symbol heap exceeds `max_affected` / `MAX_AFFECTED_SYMBOLS`, and again independently when the reverse-impact BFS hits `MAX_DEPENDENTS_WALK`. `project_impact` copies that single bit onto `ImpactSummary.truncated`. True can mean “symbol prefix only”, “walk stopped”, or both. (2) How much was left out. `ImpactSummary` counts only what was returned (`affected_symbols`, `dependent_files`, `known_tests`). There is no pre-cap matched/total to compare against, unlike `anvil_search_symbols` (`matched`, `returned`, `truncated`). ADR-142 notes that on a bounded traversal the true total is unknowable without finishing the walk the budget exists to prevent, so a bare integer would either lie or defeat the cap. Those two gaps are why GATT exists. Anvil already discloses privacy (`RedactionSummary`) and assurance (`workspace_assurance` / ADR-084 degradation). The third class — what the answer could not resolve or could not fit — is good on search and thin on the highest-stakes tools. GATT (`plans/modules/graph-answer-attestation.aps.md`, implementing Accepted ADR-142 `plans/decisions/142-graph-answer-attestation.md`) is the in-band self-attestation contract so a consumer can distinguish a complete answer from a bounded one without reading Anvil’s source. Concretely GATT (and ADR-142 §1–§5) require a shared `Attestation` on every GCTX projection, not another bool: named, separately counted bounds (section + which budget + `returned` + tagged `total` as `Exact(n)` or `AtLeast(n)`). GATT-004 is the item that stops `ImpactSummary` / `AffectedTestsSummary` from covering two budgets with one flag; `truncated` is kept one release as a derived shim (`true` iff any bound entry exists). Per-edge call-resolution fidelity (GATT-003): `call_graph.rs` already computes per-edge ambiguity then OR-s it into a caller-level `heuristic`, so one unresolvable call marks the whole caller. Cost self-report (GATT-005): `estimate_gctx_tokens` already drives the snippet budget but is not reported. The product claim is that blast-radius output can be checked. “These twelve symbols are affected” currently does not say which of two budgets stopped the walk, that three edges were guesses, or what the answer cost. GATT exists to put those limits in-band, identically across the six tools and `graph://`, as counts and closed enums only.
