## Answer (summary)

The resident graph is owned by `crates/anvil-graph-cache` (package `eddacraft-anvil-graph-cache`). That crate is the parser-free semantic-graph store for the intercept daemon: `SymbolGraph` plus derived `DependencyGraph`, incremental `update_file` mutation, trust annotation, snapshots, and `GraphRegistry` (`crates/anvil-graph-cache/src/lib.rs`, `crates/anvil-graph-cache/ARCHITECTURE.md`). At runtime it is held warm per worktree in the `anvil-intercept` daemon as `KernelGraphCache` (`crates/anvil-intercept/src/kernel_cache.rs`); ADR-084 states the MCP server is graph-less and never builds or holds this graph. Wire/node types such as `SymbolNode` / `SymbolIdentity` live in `anvil-kernel-types`; graph mutation and algorithms stay in `anvil-graph-cache`. Sealed egress DTOs and `GctxProjector` are split across two ADR-084 crates, not one. `crates/anvil-gctx-types` (`eddacraft-anvil-gctx-types`) owns the sealed, graph-free egress DTOs (`SymbolSummary`, query/projection types, `GctxError`, and the CE-5 no-leak test) and must not depend on `anvil-graph-cache`. `crates/anvil-gctx-egress` (`eddacraft-anvil-gctx-egress`) owns `GctxProjector` (`pub struct GctxProjector` in `crates/anvil-gctx-egress/src/lib.rs`), the single CE-5 choke point that builds those DTOs from the warm graph. Daemon save-time handlers in `crates/anvil-intercept/src/save_time.rs` call the projector; they do not own it. This matches the CCTX Interfaces note in `plans/modules/context-compiler.aps.md` (resident graph in `crates/anvil-graph-cache`; sealed egress DTOs in `crates/anvil-gctx-types`) and the crate split in `plans/decisions/084-gctx-graph-handle-access.md`.
