## Relationships

`truncated: bool` means a result cap bound the report (affected-symbol set or dependent-closure walk). It does not say which bound, how much was dropped, or per-edge fidelity. GATT exists so graph answers state those limits in-band.

## Invariants and applicable policy

Do not treat `truncated: false` as 'the graph is complete'. Do not fork GATT from this spike.

## Likely blast radius
