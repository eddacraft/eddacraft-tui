## Invariants and applicable policy

A caller reachable via several frontier nodes in one hop is recorded once, with `heuristic` OR-ed across all its edges. A consumer must not treat a `heuristic` caller as an exact call (GCALL-007 CALL-1).

## Likely blast radius
